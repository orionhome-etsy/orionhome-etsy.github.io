2. What Orion Studio is used for

Orion Studio is used by one person only – the shop owner – to publish Pins about the shop’s own Etsy products to the shop’s own Pinterest business account. There is no public sign-up, and no other person can connect a Pinterest account to Orion Studio.

3. Which Pinterest data we process

  • Access tokens issued by Pinterest when the shop owner connects the shop’s own account (OAuth). They are used only to act on that account.
  • Account basics of our own account (username, account ID) to confirm the connection (scope user_accounts:read).
  • Our own boards (names and IDs) to choose the right board and, if needed, create a board for a product category (scopes boards:read, boards:write).
  • Our own Pins: creating Pins for our listings and reading their status (scopes pins:read, pins:write).

We do not access, collect or store data of other Pinterest users (no followers, comments, messages, analytics of other accounts). We do not use Pinterest data for advertising, profiling or any purpose other than publishing our own Pins.

4. Where the data is stored and for how long

Tokens and Pin records are stored only on the shop owner’s own computer and own private server in Germany, which are not publicly accessible. Data is not sold, shared or transferred to third parties. Tokens are kept while the connection is active and deleted when the connection is removed. Pin records (Pin ID, link, date) are kept as long as the Pin exists, for bookkeeping of our own marketing.

5. How to revoke access

The connection can be removed at any time in Pinterest under Settings → Security / Apps (“Orion Studio” → remove). Orion Studio then can no longer access the account, and the stored token is deleted.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Contact us at the e-mail address above.
