Orion Home Studio

The in-house publishing tool of Orion Home – printable wall art sets on Etsy.

Orion Home is a small independent shop that designs printable wall art (botanical, seasonal and moody floral prints, sold as sets of three and bundles) as instant digital downloads on Etsy.

Orion Home Studio is a private tool used only by the shop owner. It prepares marketing material for our own products and publishes it to our own social media accounts.

What Orion Home Studio does

  • Creates Pins for our own Etsy listings (product image, title, description and a link back to the listing).
  • Publishes these Pins to the boards of our own Pinterest business account (pinterest.com/orionhome0527) – only after the listing is live on Etsy.
  • Reads our own boards so each Pin lands on the matching board (e.g. “Botanical Prints”, “Christmas Decor”).

Orion Home Studio does not access other Pinterest users’ data, does not collect followers’ information, does not message anyone and has no public sign-up. It is used by one person: the shop owner.
